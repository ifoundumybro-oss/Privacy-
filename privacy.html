Privacy practices depend on each server’s enabled tools and the bot’s permissions. We encourage administrators to review their settings and users to contact us with specific requests.

01 /Who operates the bot

This Privacy Policy explains how HyperGuard Systems (“we,” “our,” or “us”) processes information through its Discord bot and related moderation, security, and support workflows. The operator is responsible for the bot’s processing, subject to any responsibilities of individual server owners and other parties under applicable law.

Discord independently processes information under its own policies. For details about Discord itself, see Discord’s Privacy Policy.

02 /Information we may process

The information involved depends on the permissions granted to the bot and which features a server has enabled. Categories may include:

CategoryExamples and when used
Account and server identifiersDiscord user, server, channel, role, and message IDs; usernames or display names and relevant profile details, to identify accounts and operate bot functions.
Security and moderation eventsJoin and leave events, account age signals, suspicious activity, warnings, moderation actions, audit logs, flags, and enforcement history where supported and enabled.
Messages and submitted evidenceReports, explanations, relevant message content or attachments, and other evidence submitted or accessed for enabled moderation, anti-spam, or investigation tools. Availability depends on Discord permissions.
Tickets and supportTicket participants, submitted messages, staff responses, feedback, and transcripts when tickets or transcript features are used.
Settings and technical recordsBot configuration, server preferences, command usage, error events, and operational diagnostics necessary to maintain and protect the Service.

We do not ask you to provide account passwords or Discord authentication tokens through tickets or other bot features.

03 /How information is used

We process information to:

  • Operate features requested or enabled by server administrators, including verification, moderation, tickets, notifications, and logs.
  • Detect, investigate, and help prevent raids, spam, account abuse, and other threats to participating communities.
  • Manage reports, review flags and potential blacklist decisions, respond to appeals, and provide support.
  • Protect the reliability of the bot, diagnose errors, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not claim that every server enables every listed feature.

04 /Legal basis where applicable

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, processing may be based on legitimate interests in securing communities and responding to abuse, performance of a contract with the party using or administering the Service, legal obligations, or consent where specifically requested. The appropriate basis depends on the particular processing activity and your role. You may contact us to ask about the basis for a specific activity.

05 /When information may be shared

Information may be accessible to:

  • Authorized server staff using enabled moderation, audit, ticket, or alert features in their own communities.
  • Other participating communities where cross-server security features are enabled, which may receive relevant account identifiers, flags, or security status.
  • Infrastructure and service providers used to host and maintain the bot, acting as needed to provide the Service.
  • Competent authorities or affected parties where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to address serious threats or legal claims.

We do not sell personal information. Do not assume a report or ticket is visible only to you; authorized reviewers may access it.

06 /How long information is kept

Retention varies with the type of information, feature settings, legitimate security needs, and applicable legal requirements. Operational and configuration data may be kept while a server uses the bot. Reports, moderation records, blacklist entries, and supporting evidence may be retained while needed for security assessment, appeals, and dispute handling. Ticket transcripts may be retained according to enabled features and staff actions.

We review information for continued relevance and delete or anonymize it when it is no longer reasonably necessary, unless we must keep it for lawful reasons. Removing the bot does not automatically erase every prior security report or transcript. You may contact us to request information about or deletion of particular records.

Operator check before publication: Verify these retention descriptions against your actual database, backups, ticket transcripts, and deletion procedures. Add specific retention periods where you can.

07 /Security safeguards

We seek to limit access to data to people and systems that need it, use appropriate technical measures to protect stored data, and restrict administrative and support features by role or permission. No online service or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08 /Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the circumstances, you may have rights to request access to personal data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or to object to certain processing. Where processing depends on consent, you may be able to withdraw it. Some rights have legal exceptions, for example for safeguarding legitimate security investigations or complying with legal obligations.

Send requests using the contact details below and include your Discord user ID and enough context to locate the relevant records. We may need to verify that you are authorized to make the request before acting. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

09 /International processing

Discord and service providers may process data in locations outside your country. Where applicable law requires protections for international transfers, appropriate safeguards should be used. Contact us for information about the providers and safeguards relevant to a particular request.

10 /Children and young people

The Service is designed for use on Discord in accordance with Discord’s minimum-age requirements and applicable law. We do not intentionally request highly sensitive information about children. If you believe information was collected inappropriately, please contact us.

11 /Changes to this policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy as our bot, data practices, or legal requirements change. The date above indicates when it was last updated. Significant changes may also be communicated through official support channels, where appropriate.
